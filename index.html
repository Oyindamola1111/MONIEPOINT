MONIEPOINT PRESENTATION

History & Background

Moniepoint, a Nigerian fintech company, was founded in 2015 as TeamApt by Tosin Eniolorunda and Felix Ike. Initially, TeamApt served as a service provider for banks and financial institutions. The company later pivoted to directly serving the market, launching the Moniepoint product in 2019, a mobile money agent network. Moniepoint has since rebranded to Moniepoint Inc. and has grown to become one of Nigeria's largest business payments platforms.

Vision & Mission

Vision:

Moniepoint envisions a future where everyone has access to financial opportunities and experiences financial well-being.

Mission:

Their mission is to achieve this vision by providing financial technology solutions to businesses and individuals. This includes offering payment solutions, banking services, and other financial tools

Milestone

Moniepoint recently achieved a significant milestone by raising $110 million in funding and this made them become a Unicorn Company. This funding round, led by Development Partners International, marks a major step in Moniepoint's mission to bring financial happiness to millions across Africa, particularly in areas where traditional banking is inaccessible. Moniepoint's innovative solutions led to rapid growth. In 2019, they secured a $5.5 million Series A funding round. By 2022, they obtained a banking license from the Central Bank of Nigeria, allowing them to offer comprehensive business banking services. In 2023, they rebranded from TeamApt to Moniepoint Inc., aligning the company name with their flagship product. Their efforts were recognized when they were listed as the second-fastest-growing company in Africa by the Financial Times in 2023 .

challenges & Solutions

Technical Challenges:

Moniepoint faced issues with transaction limits and technical complexities related to POS terminals.

Solution:

They developed a system to manage POS transfers, and strengthened their site reliability process to reduce errors.

Regulatory Challenges:

Moniepoint faced issues with CBN regulations regarding licensing

Solution:

They engaged with regulations,obtained necessary licenses including the Microfinance Bank (MFB) license. they also transformed from tech platforms to regulated financial institution.

Achievements

Moniepoint became an African unicorn in October 2024, achieving a valuation of over $1 billion.

Moniepoint was ranked by the Financial Times as Africa's second-fastest-growing company in 2023.

Moniepoint also operates a microfinance bank (Moniepoint MFB) that serves small and medium-sized businesses, providing them with banking, payments processing, loans, and business management tools.

Moniepoint is considered one of Nigeria's largest business payments platforms.

Moniepoint has played a key role in expanding financial inclusion by providing access to digital financial services to millions of people, including those previously excluded from the banking system.

Conclusion

Moniepoint in not just a Unicorn,it's a symbol of Africa's Fintech resilience and SME empowerment. They give financial access to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and individuals,through a robust network of agents and digital platforms.
